- **Latent Reasoning in Continuous Space for Unified Multimodal Models** — arXiv AI / ML. Unified Multimodal Models (UMMs) achieve remarkable success on diverse multimodal understanding and generation tasks, yet they still struggle to effectively leverage language-based chain-of-thought reasoning for image generation. As a result, existing reasoning-augmented UMMs often fail to inherit the test-time scaling and fine-grained controllability observed in large language models. - **TradeWar: A Benchmark for Execution-Grounded Trade Negotiation with Adaptive LLM Agents** — arXiv AI / ML. Multi-turn negotiation remains a challenging problem for large language model (LLM) agents.
